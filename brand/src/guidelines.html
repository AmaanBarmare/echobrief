The idea

Warm dispatch

A dispatch is a report filed from where the work happened — brief, factual, sent onward. EchoBrief is a working instrument, not a showpiece, and the identity follows: warm, plain, and quiet enough that the meeting content is the loudest thing on screen.

Warm, never grey

Paper and charcoal are both warm-tinted. Meetings are human; a cool-grey product reads like a database console.

One signal colour

Ember marks what is interactive or live. Used everywhere, it means nothing — so it appears once per view.

Sound is the only metaphor

Rings, ripples, waveform bars. No brains, no sparkles, no robots, no circuit-board texture.

What this kit contains

LogoConstruction, the eight approved files, clear space, minimum sizes, and the don'ts.
ColourEmber and gold, warm paper and char neutrals, semantic status, and every measured contrast ratio.
TypographyTwo stacks — Switzer in-app, DM Serif Display + Manrope on brand surfaces — plus the Indic set.

Where the truth lives

The app implements this system in src/index.css and src/components/ui/Logo.tsx. This document describes that implementation and makes it portable to decks, email, print and partner surfaces. Machine-readable tokens ship alongside it as brand/tokens/colors.json and colors.css. If a value ever disagrees, the app wins — then fix the token file.

Typography

Two stacks, kept apart

The app is a working tool and reads in one neutral sans. Brand surfaces carry a serif italic accent. Mixing the two is the single most common way to take this system off-brand.

The italic accent

One italic DM Serif Display phrase inside an otherwise sans headline. It carries the brief half of the logotype into the copy. One per headline — two cancels the emphasis and reads like a template.

Never

  • DM Serif Display below 24px, or as body copy
  • Switzer on brand surfaces, or Manrope inside the app
  • Faked weights or skewed italics
  • Positive tracking on lowercase — that's for uppercase mono only
  • A heading set in the gradient

Interface

Five signature details

These are what make a screen recognisably EchoBrief. Everything else is ordinary, on purpose.

1 · The eyebrow

How it works

IBM Plex Mono 10.5px, +0.22em, uppercase, ember, preceded by a 22px hairline. One per section — a marker, not a decoration.

2 · The recording pill

Recording

Ember tint fill, 25% ember border, pill radius, 7px dot blinking at 1.2s. Off under prefers-reduced-motion.

3 · The waveform

Twelve 3px ember bars, staggered heights and durations. Live audio state only — never background art.

4 · Status dots

scheduled upcoming recording processing completed failed

8px. Ember is never the recording colour.

5 · The gradient bar

A 4px ember→gold rule above a section header or card. The only place the gradient touches a layout.

Buttons

Start recording Save Cancel Learn more

Radius 0.625rem, weight 500. Hover moves to ember-hi, pressed to ember-deep. Gradient fill is the hero CTA only; it carries --shadow-ember, nothing else does.

Icons, surfaces, motion

Icons — Lucide React, stroke 1.75, sized to the text beside them: 14px dense UI, 18px feature cards, 24px standalone. Never mix stroke weights in one view.

Surfaces — card fill, 1px rule border, 0.625rem radius, warm small shadow. A neutral grey shadow reads as a foreign component.

Motion — interface transitions 150–200ms ease; brand motion loops slowly at 1.2–2.8s because it signals live, not look at me. Everything has a reduced-motion off-switch.

EchoBrief Brand IdentityInterface · 07
Voice

Say what happened

Conversational, not corporate. Outcome first — lead with what the user gets, not what the system does. Confident without superlatives. Specific about India: name the languages, say WhatsApp, price in ₹.

Write

Every meeting, briefed. Bot joins, records, and files the notes before you're out of the room.

Don't write

EchoBrief is a cutting-edge, AI-powered meeting intelligence platform that revolutionises how teams capture insights!

Banned: “AI-powered”, “cutting-edge”, “next-gen”, “revolutionary”, “seamless”, “unlock”. No exclamation marks. Error messages say what happened and what to do — and never apologise twice.

Rules

Ten that break the brand if ignored

  1. One ember per view.
  2. Ember never means a status. Recording is stop-red, not ember.
  3. No cool greys, ever. Every neutral here is warm-tinted.
  4. Gradient only on: logomark, app icon, hero CTA, 4px section bar.
  5. Gold is a fill, not a text colour on light.
  6. No serif inside the app; no Manrope outside brand surfaces.
  7. One italic phrase per headline, one eyebrow per section.
  8. Never rebuild the wordmark — use the supplied files.
  9. Every motion has a reduced-motion fallback.
  10. If a token doesn't exist, add it to src/index.css and brand/tokens/colors.json together — never hardcode a one-off hex.
